(a) Structure and totals; (b) functional diversity, redundancy, inequality and the density-biomass divergences: each point is Spearman's rho with its Fisher-z interval, filled where the test met the criterion, open where it did not, with the criterion drawn as the dashed lines. The intervals give the precision of each estimate and bound an individual coefficient, not the null; the detection bound is the power calculation (Table S14). The density-against-biomass difference of each trait state is Figure S2. Functional divergence, overlap redundancy and the Gini coefficient are bounded on [0, 1] by construction, and the redundancy and concentration divergences on [-1, 1]. Their axes start above the theoretical minimum because the observed values occupy a narrow part of that range and a full 0-1 axis would compress the within-region gradient to invisibility; Rao's quadratic entropy, functional dispersion, richness and the Hilsenhoff Biotic Index, which have a meaningful zero and data reaching toward it, are drawn from zero.

> **Graphical abstract.** Trait sharing (functional redundancy) of stream macroinvertebrates weighted by density (individuals m-2) and by biomass (mg m-2) in the poorer and the better third of 30 streams in each of three Chesapeake Bay regions, ranked by biological condition (the regional benthic index of biotic integrity, B-IBI); the middle third is not drawn. Large points are medians of ten streams, small points single streams (density left, biomass right of each median), and the tinted area is the gap between the two weightings. In every region, biomass-weighted minus density-weighted sharing was higher where condition was better (Spearman rho = +0.60, +0.46, +0.43 in Valley and Ridge, the Piedmont and the Coastal Plain; false discovery rate <= 0.10): where condition was poorer, the high-density taxa, which contribute most of the density, shared more traits than the large-bodied taxa, which contribute most of the biomass (proxy: body size is inferred from the weighting, not measured per taxon here). The side that differed varied by region: density-weighted sharing in Valley and Ridge and the Coastal Plain (rho = -0.48, -0.66), biomass-weighted sharing in the Piedmont (rho = +0.42).
